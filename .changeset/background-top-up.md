A prompt never carries the routing deposit any more, so a deposit no longer costs a prompt its routing hint. `tenjin mcp` sets funds aside for routing in the background, with one `warm` call to the router that is charged nothing and carries the deposit: when it starts on a channel that cannot cover a fee (a new one included), and right after a paid call leaves the channel that low. A routing call that meets one in progress waits for it inside its own time limit, then pays from the channel. Each deposit is the smallest of the router's suggested amount ($0.03 today), the wallet balance and the per-call limit, never less than ten fees and never more than $0.25, so a big wallet no longer sets aside $0.25 at once. A wallet below ten fees is told once, with its balance, which is read only for the top-up. A router without the `warm` form gets the deposit on the next paid call for an hour, as before. The install question now says funds are set aside at most $0.25 at a time.
