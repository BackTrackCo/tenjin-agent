To keep a DeepSeek offer native, the parent sends the same Agent (or Task) call again unchanged, and it runs natively as sent, in every permission mode, with no second routing fee. Tenjin no longer rewrites a held call, which auto mode refused after its review and which could buy a second offer. The free repeat counts once per offer in the same session and workspace, until the offer expires 15 minutes after it was made, or 15 minutes after its accepted worker stopped or never started. Any other change is a new call that may be offered again with its own routing fee. The first offer's routing fee, worker inference and the 5% proxy fee are unchanged.
