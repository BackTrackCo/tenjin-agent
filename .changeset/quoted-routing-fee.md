The routing fee is the price the router quotes. Tenjin pays a quote at or below $0.003 a call, the fee every install agreed to, inside the existing spend limits, and pays nothing above it, saying why once. The router may charge less than it quoted. The install question names that ceiling as the amount you approve; other fee wording shows the quoted price, or names no amount where no quote is known.
