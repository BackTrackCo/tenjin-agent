The routing fee is the price the router quotes. Tenjin pays a quote at or below $0.003 a call, the fee every install agreed to, inside the existing spend limits, and pays nothing above it, saying why once. The router may charge less than it quoted. The install question names that ceiling as the amount you approve, and `tenjin doctor`'s routing fee row and router check, the line a routing deposit adds and the request tool's instructions name it too; only a quote above it is named by its price.
