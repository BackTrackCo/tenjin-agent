'tenjin-cli': patch
---

Tenjin no longer touches your agent's questions to you. Before, a question could be held back once and replaced by a paid lookup matched to something you had said earlier, even when the lookup had nothing to do with the question. Now the agent's questions are not sent to the router at all, so they are never blocked, never wait on Tenjin and never cost a routing fee. Your prompts, searches, fetches, delegated tasks and your answers to a question are routed as before.
