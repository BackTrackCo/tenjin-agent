Tenjin no longer stops your agent from asking you a question. Before, a question could be held back once with a paid lookup in its place, even when the question had nothing to do with that lookup. Now a suggestion is added beside the question, which you still see, and your agent is told to use it only when the question asks you for an API key, an account or paid access that the service would replace.
